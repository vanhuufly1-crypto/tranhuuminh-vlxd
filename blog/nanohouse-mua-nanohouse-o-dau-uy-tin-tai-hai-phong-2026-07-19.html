Vitratex is a thin, lightweight material made from natural fibers, designed to provide exceptional durability and fire resistance. Its structure allows it to withstand harsh weather conditions while maintaining its structural integrity. It’s also known for its excellent soundproofing properties, making it ideal for both residential và commercial spaces.

- Fire Resistant: The material is treated to prevent ignition, ensuring safety in high-risk environments.

- Soundproofing: Its density absorbs noise, creating a quieter living or working space.
